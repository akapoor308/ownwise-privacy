OwnWise

A personal home for the things you own, published by AshishKapoor30.

Organise household purchases and vehicle records. Keep original invoices, warranties, photos and service files together, track warranty and care dates, and find paper originals with private QR pouch labels.

Your records and Google backup

Records are saved on your Android device. Production requires Google connection and permission to back up your collection in OwnWise’s private Drive app-data folder. OwnWise does not access your other Drive files. Saved changes queue full backups with original documents. Uploads need internet and valid authorization. Restore a backup on another phone using the same Google account; this does not provide simultaneous multi-device editing.

Free, trial and Pro

Production starts 10 days of Pro at first completed Google sign-in, with no payment details or automatic charge. After the trial, Free supports 3 items with one invoice and one warranty card each. Pro adds more items, additional documents, service and repair histories, claim packs and themes. Annual Pro is ₹249 in India, US$7 in the United States, and the Google Play local equivalent elsewhere. A subscription starts only when you choose and confirm it through Google Play.

Closed-test builds have separate temporary access and optional Google connection. Production access is currently under review; this page does not announce a public launch.
