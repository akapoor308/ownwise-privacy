OwnWise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

OwnWise is published by AshishKapoor30. This policy applies to the OwnWise Android application, package com1.ownwise.app.

Privacy and support contact: kapoorashish213@gmail.com.

Your collection stays on your device

OwnWise stores your local profile name, item details, purchase prices and dates, product photos, invoices, warranty cards, registration notes, reminders, and repair or service records on your device. It uses this information to organise your collection, display warranty and registration dates, prepare exports, and schedule the reminders you choose.

OwnWise does not upload this collection to a publisher-operated server. It does not provide cloud synchronization or an online OwnWise account. Your local profile is independent of the Google Play account used for purchases. We do not sell your collection or use it for advertising.

Invoice recognition

Invoice images, PDF pages, and recognised text are processed on your device using bundled Google ML Kit text-recognition and language-identification components. OwnWise does not send your invoice images or text to an online OCR service. If recognition fails, you can enter details manually or store the original document without extraction.

Google ML Kit automatically collects technical information for diagnostics and usage analytics. This includes device model and operating-system information, app package and version, per-installation identifiers, performance and error information, feature events, configuration such as image format and resolution, input/output sizes, and identified languages. Google states that this SDK information is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and is not transferred by ML Kit to third parties. These diagnostics are separate from your locally stored invoice contents.

See Google ML Kit's data disclosures and Google's Privacy Policy for Google's handling of this information.

Google Play purchases

When Pro subscriptions are available, Google Play processes payments and subscriptions. OwnWise checks purchase status, verifies purchase data, and acknowledges completed purchases to enable paid features and restore access. Purchase tokens and verification data are handled by the native app; OwnWise does not send them to a publisher-operated billing server. We do not receive your payment-card details. Google retains purchase and account information according to its own policies. Cancelling a subscription is done through Google Play; deleting app data does not cancel a subscription.

Permissions and reminders

OwnWise accesses images or documents when you choose files or capture a photo through your device's picker or camera. It saves attached copies in the app's local storage. Files are exported outside the app only when you choose a save, share, or export action. Original photos may contain metadata such as location information added by your camera; consider removing that metadata before sharing originals or backups.

Notification permission is used for warranty, registration, service, and optional purchase reminders. Reminder schedules and responses are stored on your device. Notifications may display item names or dates on your lock screen according to your device settings. You can disable alerts or purchase prompts in OwnWise's notification settings and control lock-screen visibility through Android settings.

Backups, sharing, and external websites

OwnWise's automatic Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. Full backup ZIPs, exported originals, and warranty claim packs are copies you choose to create. These exports are not encrypted by OwnWise. If you save them to a cloud drive, send them to someone, or use another app to share them, that service or recipient receives the information you select under its own terms and privacy practices. OwnWise does not automatically submit manufacturer registrations or warranty claims.

Manufacturer links open only when you choose them. Those websites may receive normal browser information and any information you enter there, under their own privacy policies.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local records remain until you delete them, replace your collection during restore, clear the app's storage, or uninstall OwnWise. Deleting an item removes its associated records and attached copies from OwnWise. Exported files, camera originals, or backups stored elsewhere remain until you delete those copies separately. Google-controlled diagnostic and purchase records follow Google's retention policies.

You can view and edit your records, export a full backup, and delete your local collection. We cannot access or recover your locally stored collection. Keep backups if you want to retain it when changing devices or reinstalling.

If you contact support, your email address and the information you voluntarily send are used to answer your request and resolve the issue. Email is processed by the email service provider. Please avoid sending invoices or other sensitive documents unless necessary. We retain support correspondence while needed to handle your request and follow-up, or where retention is required by applicable law. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence; we may retain information where legally required.

Security

Your collection is kept in the app's local storage and protected by Android's application isolation and your device security. OwnWise does not add its own encryption to exported backups or documents. Protect your device and exported files from unauthorised access.

Children

OwnWise is a general product-record organiser and is not designed specifically for children. It does not operate an online registration service for children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when OwnWise's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions or requests, email kapoorashish213@gmail.com.
