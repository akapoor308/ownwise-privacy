OwnWise Privacy Policy

Updated: 19 November 2026

OwnWise is developed and published by Bhalanetra Labs. This policy applies to the OwnWise Android app, package com1.ownwise.app, from version 0.6.7.

Privacy and support contact: kapoorashish213@gmail.com.

OwnWise works offline

From version 0.6.7, OwnWise does not request Android's internet permission. Android itself blocks the app, and every library inside it, from connecting to the internet. There is no OwnWise account, server, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Everything you add stays in the app's private storage on your phone: your name, items, prices and dates, photos, invoices, warranty cards, vehicle details, service records, reminders, pouch labels, My IDs, My Documents and Family Handover. Bhalanetra Labs never receives it and cannot see it.

Information leaves your phone only when you choose to save a backup, save a file or share something. Where it goes then is up to you.

My IDs, My Documents and Family Handover

These are kept in a separate encrypted vault. You choose how it opens: your Android screen lock (including a supported fingerprint or face unlock), or a separate OwnWise PIN of 6 to 12 digits. OwnWise never receives your biometric data or screen-lock credential. The PIN is never stored; it is stretched with Argon2id and the result is also tied to this phone's Android Keystore, and repeated wrong PINs add growing delays. If you forget the PIN, you can reset it with your phone's screen lock if you allowed that, or restore a backup.

Photos are re-encoded on your phone to remove location and camera details. ID copies you export are marked as copies with the purpose and date you enter, and any areas you black out are painted into the copy. A private list of recent exports is kept inside the vault, and you can clear it.

Reminder notifications for IDs and documents say only that something needs renewal. They never show its name, number or photo. Family Handover asks you not to store passwords, PINs, full card numbers or recovery phrases, and warns you when an entry looks like one.

Backups

Backups are files you create and save where you choose, such as an SD card, a USB drive or a computer. They are encrypted on your phone with your password, using Argon2id and AES-256-GCM. If you use My IDs, you confirm its lock when backing up, and the vault's key goes inside the encrypted backup so the backup alone restores it. Without the backup password the file can't be opened, and we can't reset it.

Android's own cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for OwnWise. Backups from earlier versions, including unencrypted ZIP files, can still be restored.

Sharing files

When you share a document, ID copy, claim pack or handbook, you can add a password. The file is then sent as an AES-256 encrypted PDF that opens in any PDF reader with that password. Without a password, the file is shared as it is.

The app or person you share with handles the file under their own terms. OwnWise doesn't create public links or send anything automatically.

Google services on your phone

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card details. OwnWise checks Google Play's signature on the purchase on your phone. Google may share order details with us in Google Play Console, such as the order number, product, price and country, as needed for payments, refunds and tax.

The camera document scanner and QR scanner run inside Google Play services, a separate app on your phone with its own internet access. It may download scanner components and handles technical diagnostics under Google's Privacy Policy. OwnWise receives only the scan you confirm.

Text recognition and language identification models are bundled in OwnWise. Because OwnWise has no internet permission, they can't send diagnostics from the app.

Trial and Pro

The 10-day trial starts the first time you open OwnWise and is recorded on your phone only. No payment details are needed and it does not turn into a paid plan. Restoring a backup doesn't restart it. Clearing the app's storage removes the trial record and your data together.

Deleting your data doesn't cancel a yearly subscription. Manage it in Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Permissions

Camera and files: used only when you take a photo, scan or choose a file. Notifications: reminders you set up, never promotions. Biometrics: to unlock My IDs. Start at boot: to restore reminder schedules after a restart. OwnWise does not request internet, location, contacts or microphone access.

Notifications may show item names and dates on your lock screen, depending on your Android settings.

Earlier versions

Versions before 0.6.7 offered optional Google sign-in, automatic Google Drive backup and an online trial and purchase check. Updating to 0.6.7 removes the Drive staging files and sign-in session from your phone.

Backups those versions saved stay in your Google Drive's hidden OwnWise app folder until you remove them: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps → OwnWise → Delete hidden app data. The earlier online trial and purchase service has been shut down and all its records deleted.

Deleting your data

In Profile → Privacy and data you can delete all data on this phone, with or without your name and settings. Uninstalling OwnWise or clearing its storage also deletes everything. Because we hold no copy, we can't recover it afterwards, and support can't delete it for you remotely. Files you saved or shared elsewhere stay until you delete them.

Links and support

Links such as our website, Google Play and social pages open in your browser, outside OwnWise. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

If you email support, we use your address and what you send only to reply, and keep it only as long as needed or required by law. Please don't send documents, passwords or card details.

Children

OwnWise is a general organiser and is not designed for children. It collects nothing online from anyone.

Changes and contact

We'll update this policy if what OwnWise does with data changes. The date above shows the current version. Questions: kapoorashish213@gmail.com.
